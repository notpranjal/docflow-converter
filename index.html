FAQ

A few useful answers.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. DocFlow runs entirely in your browser. Your selected documents and generated PDFs stay on your device.

What files can I convert?

DOCX documents receive full layout-aware conversion. ODT files are converted from their text content. Older DOC files are accepted, but browser-only conversion of that legacy format depends on whether the browser can read its text.

Is there a file size limit?

Yes — each file can be up to 20 MB. This keeps local conversion fast and dependable, especially on mobile devices.

Can I convert multiple files?

Absolutely. Add as many supported files as you need, then DocFlow converts and downloads each successful PDF in turn.
