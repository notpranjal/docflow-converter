How it works

From PDF to editable text in three simple steps.

For text-based PDFs, DocFlow extracts the readable document text locally and packages it for Word.

01

Choose your PDF

Select or drop one PDF file. It stays in your browser while it is processed.

02

Convert locally

DocFlow reads selectable text and structures it into a Word-compatible document.

03

Open in Word

Your editable file downloads immediately, ready to open in Microsoft Word or LibreOffice.

Private by design

There are no file uploads in Standard Text mode. The PDF never leaves your device.

About scanned PDFs

Image-only scans need OCR to recognize characters. That is intentionally labelled as a service feature rather than producing an inaccurate conversion.
